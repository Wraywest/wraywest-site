7 Best ConvertKit Alternatives for 2026 (Honest Picks)

The 7 best ConvertKit alternatives for 2026, ranked by price and fit. Compare free plans and real costs across MailerLite, Beehiiv, Flodesk and four more.

13 min read Updated October 20, 2026 By Dwayne Lindsay

Key takeaways

  • MailerLite is the best overall ConvertKit alternative: its Growing Business plan starts at $10/month for 500 subscribers and runs near $39/month at 5,000.
  • The post ranks seven alternatives, each chosen for the single reason you would pick it over Kit.
  • Beehiiv is the best swap for newsletters, with a free Launch plan covering 2,500 subscribers and unlimited sends.
  • Systeme.io is the best all-in-one option, with a free plan covering 2,000 contacts plus funnels, a course, and unlimited emails.
  • LeadsLeap is the cheapest unlimited option at $27/month Pro for unlimited subscribers.

Some links are affiliate links. Each review says whether I used the tool myself or researched it. Full disclosure

If you are hunting for ConvertKit alternatives in 2026, it is almost always one of two reasons: the price climbed after the September 2025 increase, or the free plan’s single-automation limit boxed you in. Both are fair. Here is the short version before the detail. For most creators, MailerLite is the best swap, since it does the same core job for roughly a third of the cost. If you run a newsletter and want to monetize it, Beehiiv is the better home. If you need funnels and a course alongside email, Systeme.io wins. The other four below each fit a specific situation.

One honest caveat up front: ConvertKit, now called Kit, is still a genuinely good tool. Its free plan covers up to 10,000 subscribers with unlimited emails, which no creator email tool on this list beats, and its automations are built for exactly the way creators work. You are not leaving because Kit is bad. You are leaving because the math or the feature mix stopped fitting your business. I run a small list myself and am not a power user, so where hard numbers matter I cite independent testing rather than my own results. This is about fit, not a takedown, and the same logic applies to a 7-step welcome sequence or a run of broadcasts. My full ConvertKit (Kit) review has the deeper detail if you want it.

I build my own creator income in the gaps around a full-time chef shift, so I judge these tools the way a busy person should: what does it cost when my list grows, how fast can I get a sequence live, and does the free plan let me start without a card. Here are the seven best Kit alternatives, what each one costs in 2026, and the single reason you would pick it over Kit.

1. MailerLite: The Best ConvertKit Alternative Overall

If you want one safe answer, it is MailerLite. It does the things Kit does well, clean automations, a solid form and landing-page builder, reliable deliverability, but it prices on a far gentler curve. The free plan covers 500 subscribers and 12,000 emails a month, which is smaller than Kit’s free tier, but the paid plans are where it pulls ahead. The Growing Business plan starts at $10/month for 500 subscribers, runs about $25/month at 2,500, and lands near $39/month at 5,000.

The one reason to pick it over Kit: price at scale. At 5,000 subscribers you are looking at roughly $39/month on MailerLite against $89/month on Kit. That is $600 a year back in your pocket for broadly the same job. MailerLite also has a friendlier drag-and-drop editor than Kit’s deliberately plain style. The honest trade-off: MailerLite’s free plan is much smaller, and it has a manual account-approval step that occasionally trips up new users. For most working creators, the savings win. See MailerLite pricing to check your tier.

2. Beehiiv: The Best Kit Alternative for Newsletters

Beehiiv was built by former Morning Brew operators specifically for newsletters, and it shows in the parts Kit treats as afterthoughts: a built-in referral program, an ad network that pays you, paid subscriptions, and a real website for your archive. The free Launch plan is generous at up to 2,500 subscribers with unlimited sends. Paid plans start at $43/month for Scale on annual billing, closer to $49 month-to-month, with the Max plan at $96/month annually.

The one reason to pick it over Kit: monetization that is wired in. Kit can sell digital products, but Beehiiv hands a newsletter writer ad revenue, referrals, and paid subscriptions in one dashboard, and it takes 0% of your subscription revenue beyond Stripe’s fee. If your plan is to grow a newsletter into income, Beehiiv is purpose-built for that path. The caveat: it is a worse fit if email is just one channel in a broader business, since it is a newsletter platform first and a general email tool second. For a comparison of Kit’s commerce features, my Kit review covers what stays and what goes. See Beehiiv pricing.

3. Flodesk: The Best Kit Alternative for Design

Kit’s emails are intentionally plain, on the theory that text feels personal and lands in the inbox. Flodesk takes the opposite bet: its templates are genuinely beautiful, and it is the tool of choice for designers, photographers, and product makers who want emails that look like a brand. Note a 2026 change, though. Flodesk retired its famous flat-rate unlimited plan for new members on December 2, 2025. Pricing now scales with your list: Lite starts at $25/month, Pro at $28/month, and the Everything plan at $54/month, each at the 1,000-subscriber tier.

The one reason to pick it over Kit: visual polish. If your brand lives or dies on how things look and you would rather not fight a plain-text editor, Flodesk’s templates are in a different league. The honest caveat is that the old “one flat price for unlimited subscribers” pitch is gone for new accounts, so the value case is weaker than it was a year ago, and the automations are simpler than Kit’s. Pick Flodesk for looks, not for deep logic. See Flodesk pricing.

4. Mailchimp: The Best-Known ConvertKit Alternative

Mailchimp is the name everyone recognizes, and for a small business that wants email plus a wider toolkit, it still earns a look. The free plan is thin at 250 contacts and 500 emails a month with branding on every send. Paid plans open with Essentials at $13/month for 500 contacts, while Standard, which adds the multi-step automations most creators actually need, starts at $20/month and climbs to roughly $100/month at 10,000 contacts.

The one reason to pick it over Kit: the ecosystem. Mailchimp connects to almost everything, has the broadest template and reporting library, and bundles in extras like a website builder and basic CRM. The catch that bites creators: Mailchimp counts every contact toward your limit, including unsubscribed and non-subscribed people, so your bill can creep up for contacts you cannot even email. This is a convertkit vs mailchimp call that usually comes down to whether you value the brand and integrations over Kit’s creator-first simplicity. For a focused creator, I would take Kit or MailerLite first. See Mailchimp pricing.

5. Systeme.io: The Best All-in-One Kit Alternative

Systeme.io is the pick when email is only part of what you need. It bundles a real funnel builder, course hosting, a website and blog, and a built-in affiliate program around its email engine, all under one login. The free plan is the most complete on this list for a beginner: 2,000 contacts, 3 funnels, 1 course, 1 blog, and unlimited emails for $0. The Startup plan is $27/month for 5,000 contacts, which undercuts Kit’s $89 at the same list size while doing far more.

Pick Systeme.io if you…

  • Sell a course, coaching, or digital products with upsells
  • Want funnels, pages, email, and a blog in one tool, not five subscriptions
  • Are starting from zero budget and want a free plan that can actually launch an offer

The one reason to pick it over Kit: it replaces a stack. Kit sends great email and stops there; Systeme.io runs the whole funnel from opt-in to checkout to upsell. The honest trade-off is that its email builder and automations are more basic than Kit’s, so a pure newsletter creator gives up some polish. I dig into the details in my Systeme.io review and the head-to-head Systeme.io vs ConvertKit comparison. If the all-in-one angle fits, this is the strongest value on the page.

6. ActiveCampaign: The Best Kit Alternative for Heavy Automation

If your business runs on complex automation, branching logic, lead scoring, and CRM-style segmentation, ActiveCampaign goes deeper than Kit ever tries to. There is no permanent free plan, only a 14-day trial, but the Starter plan is competitive at $15/month for 1,000 contacts on annual billing, or $19 month-to-month. Be warned that it scales fast: at 10,000 contacts the Starter plan climbs to around $111/month, and the entry tier caps automations at five actions with no branching.

The one reason to pick it over Kit: automation depth and deliverability. Independent inbox-placement testing regularly ranks ActiveCampaign at or near the top, and its workflow builder handles conditional logic that Kit does not. The honest caveat is that this is the most complex tool here, the branching and scoring live on higher tiers, and it is overkill for a simple newsletter. Pick it when you have outgrown creator-grade automation, not before. See ActiveCampaign pricing.

7. LeadsLeap: The Cheapest Unlimited Kit Alternative

LeadsLeap is the wildcard, and I include it because the price is hard to argue with. It bundles an autoresponder, a landing-page builder, link tracking, and a traffic exchange into one account. The free lifetime plan gives you 10 email lists and 10 pages, and the Pro plan is a flat $27/month with no per-subscriber pricing at all, so a list of 500 and a list of 50,000 cost the same.

The one reason to pick it over Kit: a flat price that never punishes growth. Where Kit climbs from $39 to $89 as your list grows, LeadsLeap Pro stays at $27 no matter how big you get. The honest caveat, and it is a real one: LeadsLeap is not as polished as Kit, the interface feels dated, and the deliverability reputation is not in the same tier, so I would not run a primary newsletter business on it the way I would Kit or MailerLite. It is a smart cheap supplement or a starting point, not a like-for-like replacement. I covered the trade-offs in my LeadsLeap review.

How to Choose Your ConvertKit Alternative

Seven options is a lot, so cut it down with one question: what is the email actually for? The tool follows from that, and the price gap between Kit and the right alternative is usually big enough to matter at the end of a year.

  • You just want cheaper email that works: MailerLite. Same job, about a third of Kit’s cost at scale.
  • You run a newsletter you want to monetize: Beehiiv. Ads, referrals, and paid subs are built in.
  • You sell a course or run funnels: Systeme.io. Email plus the whole sales machine for $27/month.
  • Your brand is visual: Flodesk. The prettiest templates of the group.
  • You need deep automation and CRM: ActiveCampaign. The most powerful logic here.
  • You want the most familiar name and broad integrations: Mailchimp.
  • You want a flat price regardless of list size: LeadsLeap at $27/month, with eyes open on polish.

And the honest reverse of all this: if your list is under a few thousand, email is your main channel, and the free plan covers you, the smartest move might be to stay on Kit. A free plan that holds 10,000 subscribers is genuinely hard to beat, and switching tools costs you time you could spend writing. Leave Kit when the price or the feature mix stops fitting, not because a list of alternatives exists. My approach as a chef building this on the side is simple: do not migrate a working setup to save a few dollars unless the time math works too.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best ConvertKit alternative in 2026?

For most creators, MailerLite is the best ConvertKit alternative in 2026 because it delivers similar automation and deliverability at a much lower price, around $25 per month at 2,500 subscribers versus Kit’s $59. If you publish a newsletter and want to monetize it, Beehiiv is the stronger pick. If you want funnels and a course bundled with email, Systeme.io is the better fit.

Is there a free alternative to ConvertKit?

Yes. Beehiiv’s free Launch plan covers up to 2,500 subscribers, Systeme.io’s free plan covers 2,000 contacts plus funnels and a course, MailerLite’s free plan covers 500 subscribers, and LeadsLeap offers a free lifetime account. Kit’s own free plan is still the most generous on subscriber count at 10,000, so leave Kit for price or features, not for a free tier.

Why are people leaving ConvertKit (Kit) in 2026?

The main reason is price. In September 2025 Kit raised Creator plan pricing about 34 percent, so 1,000 subscribers now costs $39 per month and 5,000 costs $89. The other reason is the free plan’s single-automation limit, which forces an upgrade the moment you want a second sequence running. Tools like MailerLite and Beehiiv do the same core work for less.

Is MailerLite or ConvertKit better for creators?

MailerLite is better on price and has a friendlier visual editor, while ConvertKit (Kit) has slightly stronger creator-focused automation and a larger free plan at 10,000 subscribers. At 5,000 subscribers MailerLite runs about $39 per month against Kit’s $89. For a working creator watching costs, MailerLite usually wins; for a pure newsletter that needs the biggest free list, Kit still holds up.

What is the cheapest ConvertKit alternative?

On a paid plan, MailerLite at $10 per month and LeadsLeap at $27 per month for unlimited subscribers are the cheapest credible alternatives. Systeme.io’s Startup plan at $27 per month is the cheapest if you also need funnels and a course. For staying free the longest, Beehiiv’s 2,500-subscriber Launch plan and Kit’s own 10,000-subscriber free plan lead the pack.
