    Frequently asked questions

    Why a checklist instead of a 50-page ebook?
    2026 industry conversion data is unambiguous: specific 1-page checklists convert at 25-50%, while generic 50-page ebooks convert at 4-8%. The right lead magnet to the right reader beats the longest one every time. We let you pick depth (10/15/20 items) so you can match the format to your audience's attention span.
    Are these AI-generated?
    No. Every output is built from a hand-curated template library, not an LLM call. That means the structure stays consistent and high-quality, no API costs, no hallucinations, and your topic data never leaves your browser. Same input always produces the same output.
    How do I actually use the checklist after I download it?
    Three common paths: (1) Drop the PDF into your existing newsletter as the welcome gift. (2) Convert to a LinkedIn carousel (one slide per checklist item, that's why we keep them tight). (3) Use as the spine for a longer guide later. The structure is solid; expand each item into a paragraph and you have a 1,500-word post.
    Can I edit the output?
    Yes. The Markdown copy is plain text you can paste into Google Docs, Notion, or any editor. The PDF is text-based (not rasterized), so it's searchable and accessible.
    Will the same topic give me the same output every time?
    Yes. Output is deterministic. Same topic + audience + goal = same checklist. If you want different phrasing, change one input. This is intentional: it lets you A/B test versions reliably.
